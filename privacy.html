Privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains how Alpine Consulting LLC ("Alpine," "we," "us") handles information collected through this website and through the software and mobile apps we build and publish. Some apps have their own privacy page with details specific to that product; where they differ, the product's page applies.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. Your name, email, phone, business name and message when you contact us or request a demo.
  • Account and business data. When a business uses software we build, it stores its own records, such as customers, jobs, appointments and messages. That business controls this data.
  • App and device data. Basic technical data needed to run our apps, such as device type, app version, crash reports and, when you allow it, notifications.
  • Website data. Standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To provide, maintain, secure and improve our software and services
  • To respond to inquiries and support requests
  • To send messages that a business has approved to its own customers
  • To meet legal obligations

What we never do

We do not sell personal information. We do not market to our clients' customers. We do not share data with advertisers.

Text messages

If you agree to receive text messages from a business that uses our software, message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text consent are never shared with third parties for marketing.

Service providers

We use trusted providers for hosting, messaging, email, payments and analytics. They process data only on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations.

Data retention and security

We keep information as long as needed to provide our services or as required by law, and we protect it with encryption in transit, access controls and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure, and we work to fix issues quickly when found.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information. If your data is held by a business that uses our software, contact that business first; we will help them respond. You can delete an app account at any time from within the app or by emailing us.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
